# Project Title

*Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis*

---

##  Project Overview

This project analyses airline passenger satisfaction data to understand what factors are associated with a positive or negative passenger experience.

The dataset contains information about passengers, their type of travel, travel class, flight distance, delays and ratings for different airline services.

The project will use data analysis, statistical techniques and machine learning to identify patterns in passenger satisfaction and investigate whether passenger satisfaction can be predicted from the available information.

An interactive dashboard will also be developed to present the main findings in a clear and accessible way.

---

##  Business Problem

Passenger satisfaction is important to airlines because customer experience can influence whether passengers choose to use an airline again.

Airlines provide many different services during the passenger journey, including online booking, check-in, boarding, baggage handling, seat comfort, inflight entertainment and inflight Wi-Fi.

However, it may not always be clear which areas have the strongest relationship with overall passenger satisfaction.

This project will investigate the available passenger data to identify patterns associated with satisfaction and provide insights that could help an airline understand which areas of the passenger experience may need more attention.

---

##  Project Aim

The main aim of this project is to analyse airline passenger data to understand the factors associated with passenger satisfaction and build a machine-learning model that can predict passenger satisfaction.

---

##  Target Audience / Stakeholders

The main stakeholders for this project could include:

* Airline management
* Customer experience teams
* Operations teams
* Marketing teams

The results could help these stakeholders better understand passenger behaviour and identify areas where improvements to services may have the greatest impact.

---

#  Business Requirements

## BR1 — Understand Passenger Satisfaction

The first business requirement is to understand the overall level of passenger satisfaction in the dataset.

I will investigate:

* The number of satisfied passengers.
* The number of neutral or dissatisfied passengers.
* The percentage of passengers belonging to each satisfaction category.

This will provide an overall picture of passenger satisfaction.

---

## BR2 — Investigate Passenger and Travel Characteristics

The second business requirement is to investigate whether passenger and travel characteristics are associated with satisfaction.

I will analyse factors such as:

* Gender
* Age
* Customer Type
* Type of Travel
* Travel Class
* Flight Distance

This analysis will help identify whether certain passenger or travel groups show different satisfaction patterns.

---

## BR3 — Investigate Airline Service Ratings

The third business requirement is to investigate which airline services appear to have the strongest relationship with passenger satisfaction.

Services to investigate include:

* Inflight Wi-Fi
* Online booking
* Online boarding
* Seat comfort
* Food and drink
* Inflight entertainment
* Leg room
* Baggage handling
* Check-in service
* Inflight service
* Cleanliness

This analysis will help identify areas of the passenger experience that appear particularly important to overall satisfaction.

---

## BR4 — Investigate Flight Delays

The fourth business requirement is to investigate the relationship between flight delays and passenger satisfaction.

I will analyse:

* Departure delays
* Arrival delays
* Delay distributions
* Satisfaction levels for passengers experiencing different levels of delay

This will help determine whether delays appear to be associated with lower passenger satisfaction.

---

## BR5 — Predict Passenger Satisfaction

The final analytical requirement is to develop a machine-learning model that predicts passenger satisfaction using the available passenger, travel and service information.

The target variable will be:

*satisfaction*

This is a classification problem because the model will predict whether a passenger belongs to one of the satisfaction categories.

The performance of the model will be evaluated using suitable classification metrics.

---

#  Research Questions

The analysis will attempt to answer the following questions:

1. What proportion of passengers are satisfied?

2. Do passenger characteristics such as age, customer type or gender show different satisfaction patterns?

3. Does passenger satisfaction vary according to travel type and class?

4. Which airline service ratings have the strongest relationship with overall passenger satisfaction?

5. Are departure and arrival delays associated with passenger satisfaction?

6. Can passenger satisfaction be predicted using the available passenger and flight information?

---

#  Initial Hypotheses

These hypotheses are defined before completing the full analysis so that they can later be tested using appropriate statistical methods.

## Hypothesis 1 — Type of Travel

*H0 (Null Hypothesis):* Passenger satisfaction and type of travel are not associated.

*H1 (Alternative Hypothesis):* Passenger satisfaction and type of travel are associated.

---

## Hypothesis 2 — Travel Class

*H0 (Null Hypothesis):* Passenger satisfaction and travel class are not associated.

*H1 (Alternative Hypothesis):* Passenger satisfaction and travel class are associated.

---

## Hypothesis 3 — Flight Delays

*H0 (Null Hypothesis):* There is no significant difference in flight delay between satisfied and neutral/dissatisfied passengers.

*H1 (Alternative Hypothesis):* There is a significant difference in flight delay between satisfied and neutral/dissatisfied passengers.

The appropriate statistical tests will be selected after examining the data and checking the assumptions required by each test.

#  Planned Project Methodology

The project will follow the workflow below:

*1. Business Understanding*

Define the problem, objectives and business requirements.



*2. Data Understanding*

Load and inspect the raw airline passenger dataset.



*3. Data Cleaning / ETL*

Identify and handle missing values, duplicates, unnecessary columns and other data-quality issues.



*4. Exploratory Data Analysis*

Explore passenger characteristics, travel information, service ratings, delays and satisfaction.



*5. Statistical Analysis*

Calculate descriptive statistics, investigate relationships and test the project hypotheses.



*6. Machine Learning*

Prepare the data and train a classification model to predict passenger satisfaction.



*7. Model Evaluation*

Evaluate the model using appropriate classification metrics and investigate the most important predictors.



*8. Interactive Dashboard*

Present the main findings through an interactive dashboard.



*9. Conclusions and Recommendations*

Summarise the findings, discuss limitations and provide recommendations based on the evidence.

---

The project will be considered successful if it can:

* Clearly describe passenger satisfaction within the dataset.
* Identify meaningful relationships between passenger/travel characteristics and satisfaction.
* Identify airline services that appear strongly associated with satisfaction.
* Evaluate the relationship between flight delays and satisfaction.
* Statistically test the proposed hypotheses.
* Develop and evaluate a machine-learning classification model.
* Present the main findings clearly through an interactive dashboard.
* Provide conclusions and recommendations that are supported by the analysis.
